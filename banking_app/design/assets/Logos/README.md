The Horizon logo files. Every file is single-purpose; pick by ground.

- `horizon-lockup.svg`: mark and wordmark on light grounds (`surface`, `surface-raised`). Wordmark ink #0d1a16, horizon lines Horizon Green #0b6b4f, sun #ffb13b.
- `horizon-lockup-reversed.svg`: on `brand-deep` and other dark grounds. Wordmark white, lines #dcefe7 and #3ccf9b.
- `horizon-mark.svg` / `horizon-mark-reversed.svg`: the mark alone, for tight spaces.
- `horizon-wordmark.svg`: the wordmark alone in ink #0d1a16 (outlined Manrope 800), for statements and legal footers.
- `horizon-app-icon.svg`: the app icon, a reversed mark on a `brand-deep` squircle. `-foreground` and `-maskable` are the adaptive and maskable versions, with the mark inside the central 66%.

Never redraw the mark: use these files, or `HorizonMark` and `HorizonLogo` in Flutter.
