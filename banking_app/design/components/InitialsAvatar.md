# InitialsAvatar

A person's or payee's initials on a tinted circle.

**Flutter:** `InitialsAvatar('Alex Morgan', size: 40)`. The colour is picked from the pot palette by the name, so the same person always gets the same colour.

**The consumer provides:** the full name.

- Use it for people, payees and the profile button. Use `MerchantAvatar` for shops and Direct Debits.
- Two initials at most, `body-strong` at 36% of the size.
