# HorizonLogo

The Horizon logo: a sun rising over the horizon with two reflections, next to the lowercase "horizon" wordmark in Manrope ExtraBold.

**Flutter:** `HorizonLogo(height: 30)`, `HorizonMark(size: 40)`; add `reversed: true` on `brand-deep` or other dark grounds. Files: `assets/Logos/`.

**The consumer provides:** the height, and `reversed` on dark grounds.

- Use the full lockup on onboarding, the lock screen, the card face and statements. Use the mark alone where space is tight (the desktop rail, the app icon).
- Keep clear space of at least half the mark's height on every side. The minimum size is 20px for the mark and 24px high for the lockup.
- Never recolour the sun, add effects, set the wordmark in another font, or capitalise it.
